Privacy

How we handle your information.

We take your privacy seriously. This page summarises how we collect, use, and protect your data.

Information we collect

When you place an order or get in touch, we may collect your name, email address, shipping address, and payment details. We use this only to process orders, respond to enquiries, and send order updates. If you subscribe to our newsletter, we use your email only for that purpose.

How we use it

We do not sell your data. We use it to fulfil orders, improve our service, and communicate with you about your order or, if you’ve opted in, our newsletter and updates.

Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data. Payment processing is handled by secure third-party providers; we do not store full card details on our servers.

Your rights

You can ask us what data we hold about you, request correction or deletion, or opt out of marketing at any time. Contact us to do so.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.
