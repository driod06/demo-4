FAQ

Common questions answered.

Do you offer free shipping?

We offer free standard shipping on orders over a set amount. The threshold and current offers are shown at checkout.

How do I know my size?

Each product page includes a size guide. If you’re between sizes or unsure, contact us and we’ll help you choose.

What materials do you use?

We focus on natural fibres: cotton, linen, and wool. Product descriptions list exact composition for each piece.

Can I cancel or change my order?

If your order hasn’t shipped yet, get in touch and we’ll try to update or cancel it. Once it’s dispatched, you can return it under our returns policy.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, to select countries. Shipping costs and delivery times vary. You may be responsible for import duties or taxes in your country.
