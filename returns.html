Returns

Simple and straightforward.

Return window

You may return unworn, unwashed items with tags attached within 30 days of delivery for a full refund or exchange.

How to return

  • Email us or use the contact form to request a return. We’ll send you a prepaid label and instructions.
  • Pack the item securely and drop it off with the carrier.
  • Once we receive and inspect the item, we’ll process your refund or send your exchange. Refunds are issued to the original payment method.

Exchanges

If you need a different size or colour, request an exchange when you contact us. We’ll do our best to fulfil it subject to stock.

Contact us to start a return or exchange.
