Shipping

How we get your order to you.

Delivery times

Orders are processed within 2–3 business days. Standard delivery takes 5–7 business days from dispatch. You’ll receive a tracking link once your order has shipped.

Shipping costs

Standard shipping is a flat rate. We offer free shipping on orders over a certain threshold—check the cart at checkout for current offers.

International orders

We ship to select countries. Delivery times and customs may apply. You are responsible for any import duties or taxes in your country.

Contact us if you have questions about your order or delivery.
